02. Hypotheses & Telemetry Validation

Using realistic synthetic telemetry logs, we rigorously tested six potential operational friction points:

Hypothesis Premise Calculated Telemetry Metric Result Status
H1: Naive Routing FIFO chronological dispatch forces excessive empty travel. Empty Travel Share = 25.7% (119.4s / 465.0s) VALIDATED
H2: Station Bottlenecks Shift handovers cause station delays. Station Wait Consistency = 45.2s avg (stable across 24h) INVALIDATED
H3: Staging Deadlocks Fleet converges on staging zones triggering traffic jams. Blockages = 34.7% Dispatch Zone, 26.6% Aisle 1 VALIDATED
H4: Reactive Charging Run-to-depletion charging depletes peak-hour capacity. Avg Charging Threshold = 24.0% Battery VALIDATED
H5: Task Asymmetry Payload imbalance overwhelms one robot class. Task Volume = 54.6% Pallet vs 45.4% Material INVALIDATED
H6: Micro-Delays Human workers cause uniform micro-interruptions. Delay Spatial Uniformity = >61% concentrated in 2 zones INVALIDATED

03. Prioritized Engineering Roadmap

PRIORITY 1 — HIGHEST ROI

Predictive Opportunistic Charging

Resolves Reactive Charging (H4)

  • Reach: 100% Fleet | Effort: Low
  • Impact: Reclaims peak-hour availability by charging during idle windows.
  • Yield: +518 tasks (+10.4% throughput)
PRIORITY 2 — HIGH IMPACT

Proximity-Weighted Routing

Resolves Naive Routing (H1)

  • Reach: 100% Tasks | Effort: High
  • Impact: Replaces FIFO with X/Y coordinate distance minimization.
  • Yield: Reduces travel cycle time to 405.3s (+14.7% throughput)
PRIORITY 3 — SPATIAL LOGIC

Zone Traffic Corridors

Resolves Staging Deadlocks (H3)

  • Reach: ~61% Tasks | Effort: Very High
  • Impact: Enforces digital one-way corridors in Dispatch & Aisle 1.
  • Yield: Clears 61.3% of global fleet blockages.
